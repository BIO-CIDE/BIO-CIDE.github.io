💡 Which One Should You Choose?

If the budget is tight ($1,400): All California Cremation has the most affordable option. It's a brief 30-minute viewing for up to 6 people, but it includes everything you need.

If you want a proper funeral service ($4,800-$5,500): Rubidoux Mortuary is a great value with either a 1-hour or 4-hour viewing. You'll just need to get a casket and urn separately, which could add $500-$2,000.

If you want everything included ($6,905): Arlington Mortuary's package is the most straightforward. It includes the rental casket, an allowance for the urn, and even flowers. No hidden costs or surprises.

📋 Key Questions to Ask When You Call:

  1. What is your complete package price for cremation WITH a viewing?
  2. How long is the viewing time?
  3. Does the price include embalming and preparation?
  4. Is a casket or rental casket included?
  5. Is an urn included?
  6. Are there any other fees I should know about?
