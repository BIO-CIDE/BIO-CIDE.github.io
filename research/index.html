💡 Which One Should You Choose?

For the lowest cost (<$2,000): All California Cremation is the simplest ID view. For a longer, 2-hour viewing in a chapel for more people, the California Cremation Center package from San Bernardino is the best value.

For a full 4-hour viewing under $3,000: The estimated package from Miller-Jones is a strong option, but you'll need to call them to confirm the final price and purchase a casket/urn.

For a traditional funeral service ($4,800+): Rubidoux Mortuary offers great value, especially their 1-hour viewing option. You'll need to add the cost of a casket and urn.

For everything included ($6,905): Arlington Mortuary's package is the most straightforward. It includes the rental casket, an allowance for the urn, and even flowers. No hidden costs or surprises.

📋 Key Questions to Ask When You Call:

  1. What is your complete package price for cremation WITH a viewing?
  2. How long is the viewing time?
  3. Does the price include embalming and preparation? (Important for a traditional viewing)
  4. Is a casket or rental casket included?
  5. Is an urn included?
  6. Are there any other fees I should know about (like mileage, if outside their local area)?
